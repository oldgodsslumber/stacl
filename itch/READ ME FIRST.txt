CAPTAIN'S LOG — Solo Companion App
==================================

A companion app for solo play of Captain's Log: build your officer and
ship, roll dice, consult the oracle, map sectors, track NPCs, keep your
mission log, and print it all as a logbook or a fold-and-staple zine.

Made by Thatwalshguy on itch.io.

Rather not download anything? The same app runs in your browser at:
https://oldgodsslumber.github.io/stacl/
(Saves made there, on itch.io, and in a downloaded copy are all kept
separately - use SAVE FILE / LOAD FILE to move missions between them.)


STARTING THE APP
----------------
Playing on itch.io? Just press Run on the game page - nothing to set up.
The fullscreen button gives the app the most room.

Running a downloaded copy:
1. EXTRACT THE WHOLE ZIP FIRST. Right-click it and choose "Extract All"
   (Windows) or double-click it (Mac). Don't open the app from inside the
   zip - the images and the guide won't load and it will look broken.
2. In the extracted folder, double-click index.html. It opens in your web
   browser. Nothing is installed.
3. Click the "?" button at the top right for the user guide.

Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop is recommended. Firefox and Safari
work too, but a few features (like saving files) behave a little
differently.

Keep all the files and folders together. The app needs them where they are.


YOUR SAVES
----------
Your three mission slots are saved automatically - in your web browser,
not in this folder. That means:

  * Use the SAVE FILE button (top right) now and then to make a backup.
    LOAD FILE brings a backup back.
  * Make a backup BEFORE you update to a new version, switch browsers,
    move the folder, or clear your browser's history/site data. Any of
    those can leave your missions behind.


PRINTING
--------
All printing starts from the LOG tab -> EXPORT.

  * EXPORT       - your mission log as an After Action Report, plus any
                   annexes you drag in (stats, NPCs, maps).
  * LOGBOOK      - blank pages to fill in by hand.
  * ZINE         - a booklet: pick 12, 16 or 20 pages, print, fold the
                   stack in half and staple along the fold. Click
                   "HOW TO PRINT" in the zine view for the exact printer
                   settings. In short: two-sided, "Flip on short edge",
                   Scale 100% (never "Fit to page"), Margins: None,
                   Background graphics on.

No two-sided printer? In the zine view set PRINT to "Fronts only", print,
put the stack back in the tray, then set "Backs only" and print again.
Try it with one sheet first - printers differ in which way the paper
goes back in.


STREAMING AND RECORDING (HUD)
-----------------------------
The HUD button (top right, desktop only) opens an overlay window for
streaming or recording your sessions, with your camera, stats, rolls and
log on screen. The REC button records the HUD window to a video file.
Your browser will ask for permission to use the camera and to share the
window.


INTERNET
--------
The app runs from your computer, but a few things load from the web:
the display font, the game icons, and camera background removal on the
HUD. Without a connection those fall back or switch off; everything else
works offline.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
Pictures are missing / the guide won't open
    You're running it from inside the zip. Extract the whole zip and
    open index.html from there.

Nothing happens when I click EXPORT, LOGBOOK, ZINE, HUD or "?"
    Your browser blocked the pop-up window. Look for a blocked pop-up icon
    in the address bar and choose "Always allow".

My missions are gone
    They're stored in the browser you used before. Open the app in that
    same browser, or use LOAD FILE with a backup from SAVE FILE.

The zine prints too small or out of order
    Check the printer settings in "HOW TO PRINT": Scale 100%,
    Margins: None, two-sided with "Flip on short edge".
